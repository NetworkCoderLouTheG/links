server rules

01 Respect Everyone

Treat all members with respect. Harassment, hate speech, or toxicity will not be tolerated. We want a chill environment for everyone.

02 No Exploiting

Using scripts, glitches, or any form of external software to gain an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited and will result in a ban.

03 Follow Roblox ToS

You must adhere to the official Roblox Terms of Service and Community Standards at all times within the private server.

04 Listen to Admins

If an Admin or Moderator gives you a warning or instruction, please follow it. Arguing may lead to a kick or mute.

05 No Advertising

Do not advertise other servers, groups, or external links without explicit permission from the owner.
